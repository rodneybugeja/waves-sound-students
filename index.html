Critical-thinking routine

1 PredictStudents commit before seeing the result.2 Test / WatchChange one variable or show one short segment.3 EvidenceWhat exactly did we observe or measure?4 ExplainUse physics vocabulary/equations.5 TransferPredict a new case.

PhET

Do not demonstrate every control. Give a question, collect predictions, change one variable, measure, then ask students to build a rule and test an untried case.

Flash animations

Pause before the key event. Students sketch the next frame, then run the animation and annotate what changed and what stayed constant.

Videos

Use three stops: prediction before result → evidence after result → transfer to a new situation. Avoid playing a full clip without a question.

Assessment sequence

After each topic block: checkpoint slide → part quiz/worksheet → corrections. Finish with the 20-question mixed quiz.
