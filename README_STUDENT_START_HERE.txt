WAVES & SOUND — STUDENT EDITION (12 LESSONS)

FOR STUDENTS
1. Extract the whole ZIP.
2. Double-click START_STUDENT_PRESENTATION.bat (Windows).
3. Keep the PowerShell/black window open.
4. Choose the lesson your teacher tells you to open.
5. Use My Notes to type notes; they save on that laptop/browser.

FOR THE TEACHER / HOSTING
Upload the entire extracted folder to the same web location.
Give students the direct lesson page you want them to use:
LESSON_01.html through LESSON_12.html.
Each direct page opens only that lesson.

Teacher-only notes, answer-key files, detailed teacher lesson plans and teacher tools have been removed from this student package.
