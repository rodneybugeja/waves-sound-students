WAVES & SOUND V24 — LINK + OFFLINE STUDENT APP

WHAT STUDENTS DO
1. Open the web link you give them while connected to the internet.
2. Click “Make available offline” and keep the page open until it says the offline copy is ready.
3. In Chrome/Edge, click “Install app” when offered (or use the browser's Install app option).
4. After that, open Waves & Sound from the desktop/Start menu.
5. The local course works offline. External NASA/YouTube/PhET pages still require internet.

IMPORTANT
- The PWA must be hosted over HTTPS (GitHub Pages, school web server, etc.).
- Opening the HTML files directly from a folder will not activate the service worker/PWA.
- The full offline course is large (roughly 150 MB), mainly because of local videos, Ruffle and PDFs.
